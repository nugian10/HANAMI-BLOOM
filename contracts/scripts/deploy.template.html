Deploy the HANAMI BLOOM Contracts

Deploy the HANAMI BLOOM NFT contract and the $HANA token to BNB Smart Chain from your own browser wallet. No private key, no seed phrase — ever. Your wallet signs each transaction after you click.

Safety first. This page will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Only connect your browser wallet (MetaMask / Binance Wallet). If any page or person asks for your seed phrase, it is a scam — stop immediately. Deployment costs real BNB (see the estimate in step 2 before you sign anything).
Two contracts, in this order. Deploy the $HANA token first (step 3) — the NFT contract needs the token address in its constructor. Then deploy HanamiBloomNFT (step 4), call setMinter on the token (step 5), verify on-chain (step 6), and activate minting (step 7). The deployer wallet only pays gas — the owner of both contracts is Bryan's My Wallet (0x0262e8e5E4584D8AB840fD950a6E2E0A414d4097), set as a constructor argument, while mint payments and the 5% royalty go to the Binance Agentic Wallet (revenue). Steps 5 and 7 are owner-only: connect My Wallet to run them here. Minting starts OFF. Agent-only via SPACE ID Agent ID, enforced on-chain.
What the contracts do. 3,333 ukiyo-e sakura NFTs (Common Wildflower 2,000 / Rare Sakura 1,000 / Epic Moonflower 300 / Legendary Eternal Bloom 33), drawn from a deterministic deck (deck hash committed on-chain, full deck published off-chain). No free mint, no per-wallet limit. Each paid mint also mints a $HANA bonus to the minter. $HANA: max supply 1,000,000,000 — 460,000,000 treasury pre-minted to the owner at deploy, 540,000,000 bonus pool mintable only by the NFT contract. The mint price is read from the contract at mint time — it is never written on this page or the site.
